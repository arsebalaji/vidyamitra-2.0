# Curriculum provenance and content scope

Verified on 21 November 2026 against the official SCERT Telangana Class 8 Mathematics English-medium 2024–25 textbook index:

- https://scert.telangana.gov.in/pdf/publication/ebooks/8%20mathematics%20em%202024-25.pdf
- Bilingual reference: https://scert.telangana.gov.in/pdf/publication/ebooks/8%20mathematics%20bil%20-%201%202023-24.pdf

The official index identifies **Rational Numbers** as Chapter 1, followed by Linear Equations in One Variable and Construction of Quadrilaterals. The app's remaining chapter catalog follows that index, with every unimplemented chapter marked unavailable. This verifies alignment to the cited edition, not certification for a later academic year's syllabus.

The eight original lesson units cover the definition and zero-denominator restriction; equivalent fractions and standard form; number line and comparison; addition and subtraction; multiplication and division; closure, commutativity, associativity and distributivity; identities and inverses; and rational numbers between two numbers. There are 32 original exercises distributed across these units, with integer/fraction answers, hints and worked feedback. All authored answers are checked by automated tests.

No textbook scans, pages, diagrams, or verbatim exercises are reproduced. The UI, mascot, explanations and exercises were authored for this MVP. Telugu instructional wording is implemented but has not received an independent teacher or native-language editorial review. Before classroom distribution, obtain educator review of terminology, reading difficulty, curriculum coverage and accessibility. Additional regional languages must not be enabled until their complete UI and content packs are reviewed.
